# **The Grbić Lab Repository**

Welcome to the official repository for our pioneering research on **mite-plant interactions and adaptations**. This repository serves as a resource for our lab aimed at developing innovative pest management strategies and enhancing plant resistance, contributing to the future of sustainable agriculture.

---

## **Research Focus**

**Genomic Exploration**: Investigating how mites interact with plants at the genetic level.<br>
**Adaptations**: Understanding genetic adaptations of both mites and plants in response to environmental and agricultural pressures.<br>
**Pest Management**: Tailoring advanced genomic insights to inform pest and resistance management strategies.
